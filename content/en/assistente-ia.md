## Hero
Talk to your money.

Aurum's AI assistant is designed to remove friction, not control. Use your preferred AI provider, choose Full with nothing to configure, or turn AI off and keep using Aurum as a complete finance platform.

## Chat Preview
- user|I spent EUR 3.00 on ice cream.
- assistant|Recorded: EUR 3.00 expense in Fun. Should I subtract it from today's budget?
- user|I'll send you a receipt photo.
- assistant|Perfect, I will read the amount and details and prepare the transaction.

## AI Modes
- Use your own provider|If you like tuning things and already have a favorite AI provider, connect it. The platform cost goes down and assistant usage stays under your control.|tune
- Full without setup|With the Full plan, you configure nothing: Aurum uses Mistral with a European setup, chosen because it is a European AI provider and assistant data stays in European territory.|public
- Works without AI|Do not want AI? That is fine. Aurum works beautifully without the assistant: transactions, budgets, accounts and charts remain under your control.|toggle_off

## Assistant Highlights
- Natural conversation, real data|Ask how much you spent, whether a category is over budget, or where your balance is moving. The assistant answers from your Aurum data and turns daily questions into concrete actions.|/assets/screenshots/06-chart-balance.png|Aurum charts used by the AI assistant
- A receipt becomes a transaction|When you are out, you do not need to open a form. Send a receipt photo, Aurum reads the useful details and proposes the right transaction, ready to confirm or correct.|/assets/screenshots/02-transactions-list.png|Aurum transactions list

## Assistant Details
- Write like you speak|Say "I spent 3.00 on ice cream" and let Aurum prepare the category, amount and transaction.
- Receipt photos|The assistant can receive a photo, extract the key data and reduce manual entry.
- Mobile first where it matters|On mobile you can open directly into chat: enjoy charts on the computer, use the assistant when you are out.
- Always your control|AI helps, but never takes over the budget. You choose provider, plan, confirmations and whether to use it at all.
