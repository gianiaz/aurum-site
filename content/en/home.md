## Security
Your data. Your keys. Privacy by design.

Aurum encrypts sensitive data at rest: remittance text, account names, counterparties and AI keys are protected with a Master Key tied to your password.

- The clear Master Key lives only during your active session.
- Personal AI keys are stored encrypted.
- AI integration is optional and controlled by you.
