## Hero
Privacy in plain language: what Aurum protects, and where attention matters.

Aurum handles personal financial data, so protection cannot be a single marketing sentence. This page explains what is encrypted, how keys work, what happens when you use the AI assistant, and which choices remain under your control.

## Privacy Highlights
- The most sensitive data is encrypted|Remittance text, account names, counterparties and personal AI keys are stored encrypted. If someone saw the database, those fields would not be readable without the right key.|/assets/screenshots/crypted-data.png|Encrypted data in Aurum
- AI is optional and configurable|You can use Aurum without AI. If you enable it, you choose whether to use your own key or Aurum's key depending on the plan. AI questions require sending the necessary context to the selected provider.|/assets/screenshots/13-profile-top.png|Aurum profile screen

## Privacy Details
- Your password derives the protection key|When you sign in, your password is used to rebuild a key that can decrypt the Master Key. The clear Master Key is not stored permanently on the server: it only lives during the session and is used to read or write encrypted data.
- Not everything is encrypted, for practical reasons|Amounts, dates, currencies, categories and some technical fields remain readable so Aurum can filter, chart, summarize and calculate budgets. Without remittance text and counterparties, these values are less identifying, but they are still financial data.
- AI usage requires transparency|To answer questions like "how much did I spend at the supermarket?", Aurum prepares a context with the relevant data and sends it to the configured AI provider. This is optional: without AI, no data is sent to external providers for intelligent answers.
- If you lose your password, there is no magic shortcut|Encryption protects data precisely because there is no universal backdoor. If the password is lost and the key cannot be rebuilt, encrypted data cannot be recovered through a simple admin procedure.
