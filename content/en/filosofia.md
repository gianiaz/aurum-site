## Hero
Every euro needs a job.

Aurum starts from one simple belief. The idea that inspired the platform comes from *You Need a Budget*: do not just inspect money at the end of the month, decide what it should do in advance. Aurum keeps that discipline and brings it into a modern product with automation, encryption, charts and optional AI.

## Method Snapshot
Method in practice

- Give money a job|Your balance is not a generic number: it becomes rent, groceries, emergencies, travel and future plans.|Budget
- Protect margin|Categories reserve room before spending arrives.|Reserve
- Adjust without guilt|When life changes, the budget moves with you.|Review
- Ask for context|AI helps read trends and choices without replacing human control.|Insight

## Philosophy Highlights

## Philosophy Details
- Budget before reporting|Aurum helps you plan before spending, not only explain afterward where the money went.
- Flexibility without failure|A useful budget changes when priorities, income or surprises change. Moving money between categories is part of the method, not a mistake.
- Automation with judgment|Rules handle repetitive transactions, while important decisions remain visible and editable.
- AI as a lens, not a pilot|The assistant can summarize, compare and answer using real data. The final choice remains yours, with privacy and configuration under control.
